Qubit

Puzzles about quantum gates. Start with no equations, then go further.

  • Turn your state into the target state with as few gates as you can.
  • Each tile is one answer you could read out. A bigger disc means a bigger chance.
  • The colour and the little hand show the phase.
  • Not every gate on a level is needed. Choosing the right ones is part of the puzzle.
  • Drag a gate onto the qubit it should act on. For a gate on two qubits, drop it on the first one; with three qubits, then tap the second.
  • Advanced adds Bell states, measurement, superdense coding and teleportation, and writes each state out as kets. It is open from the start.
  • On a keyboard, press the key shown in the corner of a gate and then 1, 2 or 3. U is undo, R is reset, M is sound.
