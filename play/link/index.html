Pair Path

Join the matching tiles before the clock runs out.

  • Tap two tiles that show the same symbol.
  • They clear when a line with at most two turns can join them through empty cells. The line may run around the outside of the board.
  • Quick matches in a row build a combo and add time.
  • From level 3 the tiles slide after every match.
  • Keys: arrows and Enter to pick, H for a hint, S to shuffle, P to pause, M for sound.
