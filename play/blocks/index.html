Lattice

Fit the blocks. Clear the lines.

  • Drag a piece from the tray onto the board.
  • Fill a whole row or column and it clears.
  • Clear several lines at once, or clear on back to back moves, to build a streak and score more.
  • Undo takes back your last move, once for each set of three pieces.
  • The game ends when none of your pieces fits.
  • On a keyboard, press 1, 2 or 3 to pick a piece. Arrows move it and Enter drops it. Z is undo, N is new game, M is sound.
